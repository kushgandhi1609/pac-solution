Our Story

Built on quality, customisation and ethical practice.

Founded in 2008 in New Delhi.

Incorporated in 2008, PAC Solution Inc. is a noteworthy name in the manufacturing, trading and supplying of an enormous assortment of products comprising coding machines, industrial magnets, metal detectors, material handling equipment, conveyor systems and rewinding machines.

In tandem with predefined evolutions and trends laid down by the industry, our offered products are made up with extreme excellence and perfection at our well-armed fabrication unit. Each unit is reviewed sternly before being delivered to the customer's doorstep.

  • Nature of business: Trader · Wholesaler/Distributor
  • Additional: Wholesale, Retail, Import & Export
  • Legal status: Proprietorship
  • Banker: Kotak Mahindra Bank
